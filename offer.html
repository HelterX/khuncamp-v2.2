Questions about the offer

What platforms do you post on? +

We map that out on the first call based on where your audience is. Most clients run YouTube, Instagram Reels, and TikTok — we adapt to what fits your offer and the people you're trying to reach.

Is there a minimum commitment? +

We ask for three months minimum on the retainer. Content growth is a compounding channel — three months is enough to see real data on what's working.

What if it's not working? +

That's what the monthly report is for. If something isn't converting, we see it in the data and adjust — you won't hear "keep posting and trust the process" without a reason.

No pitch until the math works.
