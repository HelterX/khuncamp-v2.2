The Founder

Shawn Arrington.

Former US Army. Built a 60,000-subscriber YouTube channel from scratch in Southeast Asia. Three years into figuring out what makes content actually convert, not just perform.

The story

Shawn's background is the US Army, not media. The channel came after — built alone, from zero, covering travel and lifestyle across Southeast Asia. No team, no budget, just consistent output until it reached 60,000 subscribers.

That's the part most content agencies can't say about the people running them: he built the exact kind of channel he now builds for clients, himself, first.

Khun Camp has been built and run from Da Nang since 2022. Three years in, the focus hasn't changed — figuring out what makes content actually convert into clients and bookings, not just rack up views. Khun Camp is the operating system that came out of that, and Shawn still runs it himself.
