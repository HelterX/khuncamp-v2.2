The story

KhunShawn is the YouTube name of Rashaan Arrington. The main channel, KhunShawn, is daily reaction videos on gacha games, gaming and VTuber breakdowns, and it has grown to 68K subscribers.

KhunVlogs is the other side: travel, festivals and interviews, filmed while working from Southeast Asia, including Nomads in Paradise 2026 in Siargao and Da Nang Nomad Fest. It is also where freelance videography for brands and entrepreneurs lives.

Rashaan is the author of three Kindle books, speaks at events, and served in the US Army before any of this. Khun Camp, the content team behind the channels, is still available as a service for founder-led brands.
