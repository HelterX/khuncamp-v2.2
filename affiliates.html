What we do

The client films. We handle everything else.

Most business owners know they should post video, but a single video takes around 15 hours from idea to published. That is why they post once and stop. With Khun Camp, the client spends about 2.5 hours per video on camera, and our team does the rest.

Strategy

A 90-day content plan built around what the client sells.

Scripts and direction

We write the scripts and tell them what to film.

Editing

Shorts

3 to 5 short clips cut from every long video.

Posting

Community

Replying to comments and DMs so their audience stays warm.

Who to refer

Who is a good fit

Refer these people

  • Founders and experts such as coaches, consultants, retreat hosts and course creators who sell a real offer and want to be known for it.
  • Small creators with around 2,000 to 5,000 followers who post consistently and want to turn their audience into income.
  • Any business owner who knows they need content but has no time, no editor and no one running it.

Signs they are ready

  • They already sell something (a service, program or product).
  • They have filmed before but post on and off.
  • They do everything themselves, with no editor or assistant.
  • They have said "I need to post more" or "I don't have time for content."

Probably not a fit

  • No business, offer or income plan yet.
  • Only looking for a one-off video or a cheap editor.
  • Has not posted anything in months and is not planning to film.

When they ask

"How much does it cost?"

"It depends on what you need. Shawn goes through options on the call." Never quote a price, even a guess.

"Will it get me more clients or followers?"

"That's exactly what the call is for. Shawn will look at what you're doing now and tell you what he'd change." Never promise results.

"Do you work for them?"

"No, I'm a referral partner. I just know their work and think it fits you."

"I'm not ready yet."

"No problem. The call is free and there's no pressure. It's worth knowing your options." If they still say no, leave it and check in again later.

Rules

Program terms

  • 90-day credit. If your lead signs within 90 days of your lead form submission, you get paid. If two people submit the same lead, the first submission gets the credit.
  • New leads only. If Khun Camp is already talking to that person, it does not count. Shawn will tell you within 48 hours if a lead is already in the pipeline.
  • Paid on cleared money. Commission is paid only on payments the client actually makes. If a client cancels, refunds or does not pay, that month's commission is not owed.
  • No price quotes. Pricing is only discussed on the call with Shawn.
  • No promises. Do not promise views, followers, sales or any specific result.
  • No spam. Refer people you know or have a real conversation with. No mass messages, bought lists or posting in groups on our behalf.
  • You are an independent partner. You are not an employee or team member of Khun Camp, and you cannot sign agreements or make commitments for us.
  • Your taxes are yours. You are responsible for any taxes on what you earn in your country.
  • Changes. Khun Camp may update these terms with notice on WhatsApp. Leads you already submitted keep the terms they were submitted under.
