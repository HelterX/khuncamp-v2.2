Questions

The short answers.

Who is KhunShawn?

KhunShawn is the YouTube name of Rashaan Arrington, a gaming and reaction creator, digital nomad, event filmmaker, author and speaker. His main channel, KhunShawn, covers gaming and reactions, and his second channel, KhunVlogs, covers travel, festivals and interviews.

How do brands work with KhunShawn?

Brands can book integrated ads inside videos and YouTube Live streams, first-look reactions to a game, trailer or product, event coverage, or a speaking slot. Pricing is on request. Send the details through the inquiry form on this page and expect a reply within one business day.

What is a first-look reaction?

A video where KhunShawn reacts on camera to your game, trailer or product as he sees it for the first time, in front of his audience. Pricing is on request.

Does he speak at events?

Yes. At Nomads in Paradise 2026 he gave the talk "Augmenting Yourself: Building AI Systems Around Your Gift". For speaking requests the host covers flights and hotel, and the fee is on request.

Where do the numbers come from?

The numbers are from YouTube Studio, with the date they were taken shown on the page. They are updated about once a month. Revenue is never published.
