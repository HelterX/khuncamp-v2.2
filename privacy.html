Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Khun Camp, the content team behind KhunShawn (Rashaan Arrington) ("we," "us," "our"), collects through khuncamp.com, and how it's used. We collect the minimum needed to respond to inquiries and run the business. Nothing is sold to third parties.

1. Information we collect

The only personal information collected through this site is what you submit voluntarily through the contact form: name, email address, company or event, what you are interested in (for example brand partnership, event coverage, speaking or the Khun Camp content service), and your message. Older versions of the form also asked for a budget range, content volume goal and biggest content challenge. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall site traffic (like page views and visit duration). It's cookieless and doesn't track you individually or across other sites, so no personal information is collected through it. Optional analytics and advertising tools (described in section 3) only run if you choose Accept on the cookie banner.

2. How we use it

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry and, if relevant, to work out whether and how we can work together, typically within one business day. We don't add you to a marketing list or send unsolicited email as a result of filling out the form.

3. Third-party services

Contact form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, which delivers submissions to our email inbox. Formspree's own privacy policy governs how they handle data in transit. Site traffic is measured using Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused tool that reports aggregate metrics without cookies or fingerprinting; see Cloudflare's privacy policy for details. We use Google Analytics 4 to learn which pages and ads lead to enquiries and bookings, and if we run online advertising we may also use the Meta Pixel and the LinkedIn Insight Tag. These tools use cookies and send information about your visit (such as the pages you view, the button you click, and the ad you arrived from) to Google, Meta and LinkedIn. They only run after you choose Accept on the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time using the Cookies link in the footer. The contact forms also record which ad campaign brought you to the site (the campaign tags in the link you clicked), so we can tell which ads work. If this site is deployed on a hosting provider (e.g., a CDN or static host), that provider may log standard technical data such as IP address and browser type as part of normal web server operation. This is standard infrastructure logging, not something we actively collect or analyze.

4. Data retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably needed to follow up on the inquiry and for basic business record-keeping, and delete or anonymize them on request.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it at any time by emailing Shawn@getkhuncamp.com. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Children's privacy

This site and our services are intended for business use by adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or our tools change. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Shawn@getkhuncamp.com.

This document is a starting template and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Given Khun Camp serves clients across multiple countries (with potential GDPR/other regional considerations), we recommend a legal review before treating this as final.
