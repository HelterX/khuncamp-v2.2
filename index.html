Questions

The short answers.

01

Who is KhunShawn?

KhunShawn is the YouTube name of Rashaan Arrington, a gaming and reaction creator, digital nomad, event filmmaker, author and speaker. His YouTube channel, KhunShawn, covers gaming and anime reactions.

02

What events has he covered?

KhunShawn's crew was the on-site media team at Nomads in Paradise 2026 in Siargao, Philippines (Oct 16 to 25, 2026), filming daily recaps, five films and speaker interviews. He also did videography and interviews at Da Nang Nomad Fest in Vietnam.

03

How do brands work with KhunShawn?

Brands can book integrated ads inside videos and YouTube Live streams, first-look reactions to a game, trailer or product, event coverage, or a speaking slot. Pricing is on request. Send the details through the inquiry form on the Brands page and expect a reply within one business day.

04

Does he speak at events?

Yes. At Nomads in Paradise 2026 he gave the talk "Augmenting Yourself: Building AI Systems Around Your Gift". For speaking requests the host covers flights and hotel, and the fee is on request.
