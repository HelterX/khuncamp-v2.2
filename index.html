Common questions

What do I actually have to do? +

Film when we give you the shot list. Show up for the monthly strategy call. That's it — everything else is handled.

What's the difference between the build fee and the retainer? +

The build is a one-time project that sets up your whole content system. The retainer is the ongoing monthly engagement that runs it. Exact scope and cost come out of a quick call, not a fixed menu.

How long before content drives actual customers? +

Typically 60 to 90 days before content drives consistent inbound. The first month is building the system right so every month after compounds instead of starting over.

Is there a minimum commitment? +

We ask for three months minimum on the retainer. Content growth is a compounding channel — three months is enough to see real data on what's working.
