Who is this for?

Founder-led brands where the founder is still the bottleneck between a good idea and a posted video. If you have an offer and a point of view but no time to run the channel, that is exactly what this fixes.

What do you handle, and what do I still do?

We handle strategy and content pillars, scripts and filming direction, editing and production, posting and scheduling, and replies to comments and DMs in your voice. You film. About 2.5 hours of your own time per video, plus 3.5 hours working together, and we take the rest.

Do you work with founders outside Da Nang?

Yes. The system runs remotely: you film, we direct, edit, and post. We work with founders across Southeast Asia, including Vietnam, Thailand, and the Philippines.

What platforms do you post on?

We map that out on the first call based on where your audience is. Most clients run YouTube, Instagram Reels, and TikTok. We adapt to what fits your offer and the people you're trying to reach.

Is there a minimum commitment?

We ask for three months minimum on the retainer. Content growth is a compounding channel. Three months is enough to see real data on what's working.

What happens on the first call?

A short call where we look at your channel, your offer, and your audience, then tell you exactly how we'd run it. Nobody talks price until the math works.
