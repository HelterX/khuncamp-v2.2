The paid test

Ten small promotions, $56 in total

We ran YouTube Studio promotions with the Audience growth goal at $5 to $10 each, always on clips or videos that were already performing. Ten promotions cost $56.43 and brought 2,569 subscribers, about $0.022 each.

What we learned from 94 promotions

Three things that moved the cost

$0.045

Goal matters most

Across all 94 promotions, the Audience growth goal averaged $0.045 per subscriber. The Video views goal averaged $12.07 per subscriber for about the same cost per view.

$0.021

Small beats big

In 2026, promotions of $6 or less cost $0.021 per subscriber, while $15 to $25 promotions cost $0.055.

$0.013 to $0.034

Content type matters

Reaction clips with a clear punchline (Mario, Spider-Man, MLBB, HSR, ZZZ) cost $0.013 to $0.034 per subscriber. Essay-style videos got zero subscribers.

After the ads

Did the views keep going?

Partly. The promotions seeded the clips, and most of each clip's views came from outside the ad. But the individual clips levelled off once the ad finished. The channel as a whole held its new level, at about 17K views a day for more than two weeks after the spike, against 3.3K a day before.

What else drove the growth

Not only ads. About 85% of views were not paid

Volume

387 videos published in the 28-day window, against a usual 39 to 90.

Shorts

The Shorts feed delivered 37.7% of all views (179K). One Short, a Limbus Company fan-levels reaction, reached 41.7K views.

Search and browse

Search brought 16.6% of views and browse features 18.0%, so the growth was not only paid.

Series

New Genshin cutscene reaction videos earned about 2.7K to 3K views each in their first day, roughly 8x to 10x a normal new upload.

Read this before you compare

What these numbers do and don't say

  • These are the results of one gaming reaction channel, on its own audience. They are one channel's experience, not a guarantee of what any other channel will see.
  • Cost per subscriber counts subscribers attributed to the promotion. Subscriber quality is still being measured.
  • The individual promoted clips plateaued after their ads ended. What held was the channel as a whole.
  • Five promotions are still running, so these figures will be updated after Oct 19.
