Who we are.

One line

Khun Camp is a content operating system for founder-led brands.

Short bio

Khun Camp runs content for founder-led brands: strategy, scripts, filming direction, editing, posting and community replies, handled by one team for one monthly fee. The company works from Southeast Asia and ran the on-site media team at Nomads in Paradise 2026 in Siargao.

Founder

Khun Camp was founded by Shawn Arrington, who still runs it himself.
