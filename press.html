KhunShawn is a gaming and anime reaction creator, digital nomad, event filmmaker, author and speaker.

Short bio

KhunShawn is the YouTube name of Rashaan Arrington. His channel posts daily reaction videos on gacha games, gaming and VTuber breakdowns, including Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero, Mobile Legends, Honor of Kings and Limbus Company. He works from Southeast Asia, led the on-site media team at Nomads in Paradise 2026 in Siargao, gave a talk there on building AI systems around your gift, and is the author of three Kindle books. He is a US Army veteran.
