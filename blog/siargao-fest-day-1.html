Day 1: Opening night by the water

Siargao Nomad Fest opened on Friday, Oct 16 with an opening party by the water. Our crew was filming from the start.

Siargao Nomad Fest opened on Friday, Oct 16 with an Opening Party by the water. Khun Camp was on site from the start, filming the festival and meeting local business owners.

How the night started

The festival opened at a beachfront venue on the island, with the sea a few steps from the party. A screen listed the partners supporting the festival, and Human Bingo gave everyone a reason to start talking: a card of prompts, and a pen to tick off each person who matched one.

On the crew side

Our crew filmed on handheld cameras and a phone from the first hour, so the opening night is on tape.
