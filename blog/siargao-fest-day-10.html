Day 10: Closing day: one last island trip and the Closing Party

Sunday, Oct 25 was the final day of Nomads in Paradise 2026 in Siargao, with optional island trips and the Closing Party.

Sunday, Oct 25 was the last day of Nomads in Paradise 2026 in Siargao. With the talks, the wellness day and the beach day behind them, guests had one more day on the island and one last night together.

One last day on the water

Three optional day trips left from the General Luna port: a swim at Sohoton Cove, a tri-island hopping trip, and a land tour with a visit to Sugba Lagoon. They ran from the morning until late afternoon.

The Closing Party

At 7:00 in the evening the festival came together one more time for the Closing Party on a rooftop, the official close of Nomads in Paradise 2026.

Ten days in one place

The festival ran from Oct 16 to Oct 25: an opening party, a fitness day, island hopping, four conference days, a wellness day and a beach day. Every day has its own recap on this site.
